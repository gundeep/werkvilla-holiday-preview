Kerstlicht subtle

Dutch working name: “Kerstlicht subtiel”

The app exactly as tenants know it, with a few small festive touches added.

Interactive prototype with fictional tenants and tickets, English interface.

On a phone

Today’s app first, then the same screen with Kerstlicht subtle. Tap an image to open it full size.

Up close

The top of the phone screen, enlarged: fairy lights under the top bar, a small star on the logo, the seasonal line under the greeting, and gold stars and a little snow on the villa photo.

On a computer

What’s added

  • A fine string of warm fairy lights along the bottom of the top bar (on a computer: under the logo in the sidebar), twinkling gently.
  • A tiny gold star on the Werkvilla logo.
  • One quiet line under the greeting: “Warm wishes from Werkvilla” (in Dutch: “Fijne feestdagen, namens iedereen van de Werkvilla”).
  • Three small gold stars hanging from the top of the villa photo, and a few slow, faint snowflakes.
  • Optionally, the normal app icon with a small gold star.
  • For people who prefer less motion (a phone setting), everything stays still and there is no snow.

What stays the same

  • Colours: the same cream, black and gold. The touches use the app’s own gold, plus a warm-white glow for the bulbs.
  • Fonts and layout: the same fonts, sizes and spacing. The only shift is the one new line under the greeting, which moves the title down a little; the “New report” button, the reports and the menu are otherwise where they are today.
  • Photos: each villa keeps its own daytime photo and its usual name.
  • Buttons and menu: the same black buttons and the same bottom menu and sidebar.

App icon

The normal Werkvilla icon with a small gold star, in light and dark mode. Keeping today’s icon is just as possible.
